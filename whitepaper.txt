FANTRADE — White Paper Draft v2.0 
1. Abstract 
Fantrade is a football activity exchange designed to connect football fandom with market-style digital assets and 
performance-based participation. Rather than representing ownership of a football player or coach, Fantrade 
creates digital Activity Assets associated with the football activities and performance of eligible players and coaches. 
Each Activity Asset is represented within Fantrade by 10,000,000 shares. Users can acquire, hold, sell, trade, and use 
eligible shares in Fantrade experiences such as FanPlay. 
The platform uses compact F-tickers to identify Activity Assets in the exchange. For example, Cristiano Ronaldo's 
Activity Asset may use FCR7, while Erling Haaland may use FHLND. The ticker is a short market identifier; the 
underlying asset record retains the full player or coach identity. 
$FTR is the native utility and settlement token of the Fantrade ecosystem. It is used for applicable purchases, 
trading-related flows, listing fees, FanPlay settlement, and other platform-defined utilities. 
Fantrade combines an exchange, ownership accounting, football performance data, FanPlay prediction mechanics, 
user-created listings, reference valuation, lister incentives, and an eventual blockchain settlement layer. 
2. Introduction 
Football generates enormous quantities of real-world activity: appearances, goals, assists, cards, minutes, shots, 
saves, passes, defensive actions, team results, and other measurable events. Fans already follow these events as if 
they were market movements, but conventional football products rarely connect that attention to a persistent 
ownership and exchange layer. 
Fantrade is designed around that connection. The platform transforms football activity into a structured digital 
market experience without claiming that users own the underlying footballer, coach, club, contract, image rights, 
employment relationship, or sporting rights. 
The objective is to build a market where football activity can be discovered, valued, traded, and used in interactive 
experiences. 
3. The Fantrade Concept 
3.1 Activity, not the person 
Fantrade does not tokenize football players or coaches. It creates digital Activity Assets based on the football 
activities and performance of eligible players and coaches. 
A Fantrade Activity Asset therefore represents a defined digital market relationship to football activity. It does not 
represent equity in the person, employment rights, image rights, transfer rights, contract rights, club ownership, or 
legal ownership of the player or coach. 
3.2 Core experience 
1. Discover an eligible Activity Asset. 
2. Review its Fantrade Reference Valuation, market information, football data, and available shares. 
3. Acquire or trade its shares using $FTR. 
4. Hold shares as an ownership position within Fantrade. 
5. Use eligible shares in FanPlay predictions.

6. Receive or incur FanPlay settlement based on the configured prediction rules and verified football events. 
7. Trade shares again through the Fantrade Exchange subject to normal market and reservation rules.

4.1 Share supply 
Each Activity Asset has a fixed conceptual supply of 10,000,000 shares within the Fantrade model. Secondary-
market trading transfers existing shares; it does not create new shares. 
4.2 Coaches 
Coaches are first-class Activity Assets rather than a secondary category. Their Activity Assets can use the same 
exchange, ownership, listing, FanPlay, and fee infrastructure, subject to the prediction types and football data 
available for coaching activity.

6. Reference Valuation 
Fantrade may provide a Fantrade Reference Valuation for each Activity Asset. This is an indicative platform 
valuation intended to provide a starting reference for discovery, listing, and market context.

The valuation may be AI-assisted and can incorporate football activity, performance data, player or coach context, 
market conditions, historical information, and configurable valuation methodology. 
A Reference Valuation is not necessarily the trading price. Actual secondary-market prices are determined by orders 
and trades in the Fantrade Exchange. 
The valuation methodology, model governance, update frequency, confidence presentation, and human review 
process remain subject to implementation and validation.

8. Lister Economics and Vesting 
8.1 Vesting 
A lister selects a vesting period for the claimed allocation. The minimum proposed vesting period is one year.

At the end of the selected period, the lister's 30% eligible trading-fee participation ends. Fully vested shares remain 
the lister's Fantrade shares, subject to normal platform rules. 
8.2 Trading limit 
A lister may trade up to 1% of their original allocation per day, subject to the shares actually vested and available 
and all normal exchange reservation rules. 
For a 500,000-share allocation, 1% of the original allocation is 5,000 shares per day. For a 1,000,000-share 
allocation, it is 10,000 shares per day. 
8.3 Trading-fee participation 
The lister receives 30% of eligible trading fees generated by the listed Activity Asset's shares, paid monthly, 
throughout the selected vesting period. 
Eligible trading fees are fees generated by trading the relevant Activity Asset. Listing fees, withdrawal fees, 
administrative fees, and unrelated platform fees are not automatically included.

9. Fan Points and the Pre-Airdrop Lister Program 
Fantrade uses Fan Points (FP) across its ecosystem. The lister program does not introduce a separate 'Lister Points' 
system. 
Before the Airdrop snapshot, eligible listers can accumulate FP based on the value and contribution of the players or 
coaches they list. The exact formula remains configurable and subject to economic testing. 
Once the Airdrop snapshot occurs, historical lister FP accumulation stops permanently. The historical lister program 
is therefore a pre-Airdrop program. 
Pre-Airdrop lister rewards are funded from the Airdrop allocation, not from Ecosystem & Development.

14. FanPlay 
FanPlay is Fantrade's prediction and performance layer. Users do not stake $FTR as the primary FanPlay stake. They 
stake eligible Activity Asset shares they already own. 
14.1 Core flow 
8. Select an eligible Activity Asset. 
9. Select a match. 
10. Select a FanPlay market. 
11. Choose prediction options. 
12. Select the number of available shares to stake. 
13. Review potential FP gain/loss. 
14. Activate FanPlay. 
15. Shares become locked while the FanPlay is active. 
16. Football events are evaluated using authoritative normalized data. 
17. Final FP is calculated. 
18. FP is converted to $FTR using the configured conversion. 
19. Locked shares are released after settlement.

14.2 Share locking 
A user's owned shares are divided into available and locked shares. Locked shares cannot be sold, transferred, or 
used in another FanPlay until the relevant reservation is released. 
14.3 Prediction economics 
Each prediction option has a success FP value and a failure FP value. 
Option FP = configured outcome FP × staked shares 
Total FP = sum of all selected option FP results 
Current conversion: 1,000 FP = 1 $FTR. 
Example: +5 FP × 500 shares = +2,500 FP = +2.5 $FTR. A failed prediction of −5 FP × 500 shares = −2,500 FP = −2.5 
$FTR. 
The platform must support negative settlement. The final collateral, maximum-loss, negative-balance, or 
settlement-risk policy remains an explicit economic parameter and must not be silently assumed.

14.6 Settlement 
FanPlay uses a lifecycle such as DRAFT → ACTIVE → LIVE → PENDING_SETTLEMENT → SETTLED, with exception 
states including VOID, CANCELLED, SUSPENDED, and DISPUTED. 
Activation and settlement must be transactional and idempotent. Final settlement uses authoritative final football 
data. Live FP can be provisional and may change before final settlement.

15.2 Real order book 
The order book must represent actual user orders. Synthetic depth, random price movement, fake order volume, 
and generated market charts are not part of the production model. 
15.3 Trade execution 
A trade execution should atomically coordinate order matching, ownership transfer, $FTR movement, fees, 
reservations, fill creation, order status updates, and immutable ledger entries. 
15.4 Market price 
The displayed market price is derived from actual trades. Historical price charts and statistics are derived from 
recorded executions rather than synthetic values. 
16. Ownership and Share Accounting 
Fantrade treats ownership accounting as a core financial state. 
For every Activity Asset: 
Total supply = treasury/custody shares + user holdings + other explicitly defined custody states. 
For every user holding: 
Owned shares = available shares + locked shares. 
Share reservations are authoritative for locked shares. Exchange orders and FanPlay reservations must coordinate 
so that the same shares cannot be committed twice. 
16.1 Centralized source of truth 
The initial Fantrade implementation uses PostgreSQL as the operational source of truth. Financial and ownership 
state is not stored as authoritative client-side LocalStorage data. 
16.2 Future blockchain compatibility 
Domain services and repositories are designed so blockchain settlement can be introduced later without replacing 
the product's core business logic. 
17. Fees and Economic Flow 
Fantrade can charge configurable trading and platform fees. Fees must be explicit, auditable, and represented in the 
ledger.

The proposed lister participation is 30% of eligible trading fees generated by the corresponding Activity Asset during 
the lister's selected vesting period. 
The remaining eligible fee share is retained according to Fantrade's fee policy, which may support operations, 
treasury, liquidity, ecosystem development, or other approved uses. 
The 2% listing-payment burn is a supply-reduction mechanism, not ordinary platform revenue.

19. Dream Club 
Dream Club allows users to create a virtual club using Activity Assets they actually hold. 
A user selects 11 eligible players and a bench from their available holdings, subject to configured roster rules. The 
club may include a coach Activity Asset. 
The club's FanPlay exposure is derived from the selected holdings rather than from fictional ownership. 
Individual player/coach outcomes accumulate into an Accumulated FP value. A configurable team boost can then be 
applied. 
The exact team-boost formula remains an open economic parameter.

22. Chiliz Integration 
Chiliz is a potential blockchain ecosystem for Fantrade's future on-chain infrastructure. The eventual integration

25. Legal and Regulatory Considerations 
Fantrade should be structured and reviewed with the understanding that economic substance matters more than 
terminology. 
Calling an instrument an 'Activity Share' or assigning it an F-ticker does not, by itself, determine whether the 
instrument is regulated or whether football-related rights are implicated. 
Fantrade should not represent that users own football players, coaches, clubs, contracts, image rights, transfer 
rights, or sporting rights unless legally authorized and contractually supported. 
Before launch, Fantrade should obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice covering token issuance, trading 
functionality, rewards, fee participation, consumer protection, advertising, KYC/AML obligations where applicable, 
data licensing, intellectual property, football-body permissions, tax treatment, and blockchain deployment. 
The product should accurately describe its actual economic and technical functionality and avoid relying on 
terminology as a substitute for legal analysis.

28. Conclusion 
Fantrade is designed as a new category of football market infrastructure: a platform where football activity 
becomes a structured digital asset experience. 
The core distinction is intentional. Fantrade does not claim to tokenize or sell football players. It creates Activity 
Assets associated with football activities and performance, represented through a compact market ticker system 
such as FCR7 for Cristiano Ronaldo and FHLND for Erling Haaland. 
Users can hold and trade Activity Shares, use eligible shares in FanPlay, participate in Dream Clubs, and interact with 
the broader $FTR economy. 
The combination of real ownership accounting, an exchange, verified football data, configurable prediction 
mechanics, user-created listings, Reference Valuation, lister economics, and a future blockchain settlement layer 
forms the foundation of the Fantrade ecosystem. 
The long-term objective is to make football activity continuously discoverable, tradable, and interactive while 
maintaining a clear distinction between digital Fantrade assets and the real-world people whose football activity 
generates the underlying data.
